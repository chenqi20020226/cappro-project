02 / Motivation

Abstract

Vision-language-action (VLA) models have demonstrated strong manipulation capabilities, yet adapting them to previously unseen tasks often requires additional robot demonstrations and fine-tuning. CapPro explores a different setting: a single egocentric human demonstration video provides task-specific guidance for a pretrained VLA policy. The framework learns compact, phase-aware video representations and integrates the resulting guidance into robot action conditioning, without relying on embodiment-aligned human-to-robot trajectories or target-task robot fine-tuning.

03 / Approach

Method

01

Phase-Aware Human Video Representation

Converts the human demonstration into a compact visual representation, preserving task-relevant spatial and temporal information. Context-dependent queries learn execution-stage information using phase-caption supervision during training.

02

Video-Conditioned Adaptation

Combines video features and query-derived guidance with the robot observation and language instruction, conditioning the pretrained VLA policy for action generation on an unseen manipulation task.

One-shot inference. At deployment, the policy receives the current robot observation, a task instruction, and one human demonstration video. Phase captions, target-task robot demonstrations, and target-task fine-tuning are not required.
